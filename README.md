# The Gospel of John: a visual tour

A single-page study site for Edexcel A level Religious Studies, Paper 3 (New Testament Studies).
Draft 1, October 2026.

## What is in it

| Room | Covers | Specification topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | Landing page, a chapter-by-chapter ruler of the signs and sayings, how to use the tour | |
| Author and purpose | Who wrote John, when, where and why; timeline; map of the eastern Mediterranean | 3.2 |
| The Prologue | John 1:1-18 in full; hymn, overture or later addition; Gnostic, Greek, Jewish or Essene | 2.1 |
| 'I am' sayings and signs | The seven signs, the saying tied to each, map of Galilee and Judea | 2.2 and 2.3 |
| Theology | High Christology, symbolism on two levels, the cross as glory, eternal life, the Spirit | draws on 2.1-2.3 and 3.2 |
| Glossary | 65 terms, each linked to and from the text | |

## Turn on the website (GitHub Pages)

1. In this repository, open **Settings > Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to *Deploy from a branch*, pick the `main` branch and the `/ (root)` folder, then save.
3. After a minute or two the site is live at <https://nirshaki-cell.github.io/the-gospel-of-john/>.

To link straight to a room, add its name to the address: `#author`, `#prologue`, `#signs`, `#theology` or `#glossary`.
Individual sections work too, for example `#sign-7` or `#prologue-influences`.

## How it is built

- Everything is inside `index.html`: text, styles, script, fonts, maps and pictures. It loads nothing from other websites, so it works on a school network and collects no data.
- It works without JavaScript (all rooms show on one long page) and prints as one document from the browser's print command.
- It follows the reader's light or dark setting, and the **A+** button enlarges the text and spacing.

- **Content**: the department's own notes on the Prologue, the 'I am' sayings and the Gospel of John, and the Pearson Edexcel specification published for September 2026.
- **Bible text**: World English Bible British Edition (public domain). Every quotation was inserted from the source text by script and checked against it. The Edexcel anthology prints the NIV, so wording differs slightly; the Start page lists the differences that matter.

- **Typefaces**: Marcellus, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Alegreya, embedded under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1.

## Points for the teacher to check

- **Seven signs.** The specification lists seven, including walking on water. The table in the department's 'Studies in the New Testament' notes lists six, so sign 5 here is built from the specification and the note on John 6:20.
- **Signs 1 to 3 have no 'I am' saying.** The page says so and gives each a key word (glory, life, work) instead. Those key words are editorial.
- **Evaluation points.** Most strengths and weaknesses come from the notes. A few were written to complete a balanced pair: the weaknesses of the community view of authorship, the limits of a symbolic reading, and the reply to the 'exclamation' reading of John 20:28.
- **Verses quoted beyond the notes**: John 5:17-18 (the Sabbath dispute after sign 3) and John 19:17 (caption to the El Greco). The Greek on the landing page is the opening of John 1:1.
- **Questions** are written in Edexcel style and are not past-paper questions. Mark tariffs follow the Paper 3 pattern in the notes (8, 12, 20, 30).
